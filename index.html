Sizing, care & delivery

Everything to know before you order.

Sizing

Pieces are made to fit you. Send your bust, waist, hips and preferred length, or your usual size, and we'll confirm the best fit before we start.

Care

Hand wash cold with mild soap, separately for the first few washes. Dry in the shade, iron inside out on low heat, and avoid bleach or scrubbing the drawn artwork.

Delivery & returns

Delivery time and fees are confirmed when you order. Each piece is one of a kind, so returns are accepted only for faults. Message us within 48 hours if anything is wrong.
